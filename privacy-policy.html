NexPOS Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 22, 2026

NexPOS ("NexPOS", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the information you provide when using the NexPOS mobile application ("App").

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected through NexPOS, how that information is used, how it is protected, and the choices available to users.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use NexPOS, the App may process the following types of information:

Account Information

  • Name and account identification information
  • Email address
  • Phone number, where provided
  • Username
  • Password or authentication information
  • User role, such as business owner or employee

Business Information

Business owners may enter information about their business, including business name, branch information, business settings, and other information necessary to operate the application.

Sales and Inventory Information

NexPOS may process information entered by users to manage business operations, including:

  • Products and product details
  • Stock and inventory information
  • Sales transactions
  • Purchase and supplier information
  • Customer information
  • Credit sales and payment information
  • Expenses and business records
  • Receipts and transaction records

Device and Technical Information

The App and its supporting services may process technical information necessary to operate, secure, maintain, and troubleshoot the service. This may include information such as device type, operating system, application version, network information, and technical error or diagnostic information.

2. How We Use Information

Information processed through NexPOS may be used to:

  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Authenticate users and protect accounts
  • Provide sales and point-of-sale functionality
  • Manage products and inventory
  • Manage customers and suppliers
  • Process and record business transactions
  • Manage employee access and permissions
  • Synchronize information between supported devices and services
  • Provide receipts and transaction records
  • Maintain application security
  • Detect and troubleshoot technical problems
  • Improve the reliability and functionality of NexPOS

3. Offline Storage and Synchronization

NexPOS may provide offline functionality so that certain business operations can continue when an internet connection is unavailable.

Information stored locally on a device may be synchronized with the NexPOS server when an internet connection becomes available, depending on the functionality enabled in the App.

4. Sharing of Information

NexPOS does not sell users' personal information.

Information may be processed or disclosed when reasonably necessary to:

  • Provide and operate NexPOS services
  • Store and synchronize application data
  • Maintain application security
  • Provide technical infrastructure and hosting services
  • Comply with applicable laws or valid legal requests
  • Protect the rights, safety, and security of users and the service

Business owners and authorized employees may also have access to business information according to the roles and permissions configured within NexPOS.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information processed through NexPOS against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide NexPOS services, maintain business records, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce applicable agreements.

Business owners are responsible for ensuring that information entered into NexPOS is accurate and appropriate for their business operations.
