7. Account and Data Deletion

The App provides an account deletion option through the business owner's profile. Only authorized business owners may use this in-app account deletion option. Employees are not permitted to delete their own accounts through the App.

To delete an account using the App, the business owner should sign in, open the owner's profile, and use the available account deletion option. The App may require confirmation before processing the deletion.

Users who need help with account deletion or who wish to request deletion of personal information may contact us using the email address below. Employees may contact us to request assistance with their personal information even though they cannot delete their accounts directly through the App.

Account and Data Deletion Requests

Account deletion may result in the deletion of account-related information. Certain business transaction records, including sales, payments, invoices, inventory records, and audit records, may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate business recordkeeping, security, fraud prevention, or dispute resolution.

Where information must be retained, it will be handled in accordance with applicable legal requirements and the purposes for which retention is necessary.

Uninstalling the App from a device does not itself delete an account or information stored on the backend. Users should use the available deletion process or contact us to request assistance.
